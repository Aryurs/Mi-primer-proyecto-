## Script for ElevenLabs

This prayer is for those going through deep exhaustion, when your strength seems to fade away.

Let us pray.

Sovereign and eternal God, my sustainer, in the mighty name of Jesus Christ, we come before you with honesty. We do not come pretending to have strength we do not have; today we come weary, discouraged and very weak. We are not talking about the tiredness of one hard day, but about a weariness that goes beyond the physical and makes everything feel like a burden, while deep inside we feel ourselves slowly wearing down.

Lord, you encourage us through your Word, for you say: "Come to me, all you who are weary and burdened, and I will give you rest." Thank you, Lord, for that Word. We know that you love us without measure and that you are kind toward our weaknesses. You do not ask more of us than we can give, because you know our limits. Today we ask for your help, because with our own efforts we cannot change our reality.

Heavenly Father, King of life, work in us through your Holy Spirit; uproot every invisible weight we carry each day, and release a mantle of complete restoration over our lives. Father, take dominion and authority; give life to our mortal bodies, nourish our spirits and dissolve all heaviness. We give you glory and honor, because we believe you are working, you are moving, and you are carrying out your work.

Thank you, Lord, because you are in control.

In the mighty name of Jesus. Amen.
